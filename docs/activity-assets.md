# BLESS event covers, photo v2

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool for this project (not real event documentary photos). The six selected images were resized to 960×640 and saved as JPEG quality 82. The images live in backend public/activities for admin previews and hosting. The mini-program fetches event covers from that backend instead of bundling duplicate files into its main package. The original generated PNG assets remain on the backend for compatibility.

Prompt common direction: photorealistic-natural; one landscape 3:2 editorial lifestyle photograph for a mobile BLESS event cover. Warm soft natural light, restrained ivory/olive/honey palette, realistic lived-in materials, believable imperfections, candid comforting magazine photography. Central subjects survive a 16:9 crop, unobtrusive lower edge for UI text. No lettering, logos, watermarks, borders, collage, illustration, or interface.

Subject prompts:
- little-joys: quiet morning; steaming ceramic tea cup on a wooden windowsill, small glass vase of pale yellow flowers, sun through linen curtains in a cozy apartment.
- city-walk: peaceful tree-lined East Asian neighborhood; late afternoon sunlight, small cafes and leafy pavement, two adult friends walking away in the middle distance, no legible shop signs.
- taste-of-home: home-cooked East Asian dinner; rice, stir-fried vegetables, dumplings and chopsticks on a modest wooden table, inviting home, no people.
- second-life: beloved analog camera with worn leather strap next to old books and a repaired ceramic vase, real wooden shelf, patina and everyday texture, no brands.
- hobby-corner: cozy apartment window; acoustic guitar, small desk with half-finished watercolor sketch, brushes in a ceramic cup, green houseplant, no readable text.
- kind-wishes: close candid act of kindness; realistically proportioned hands and forearms of two adult friends passing chamomile flowers tied with ribbon across a cafe table, cups in soft background, simple natural gesture, no card or text.
